Momentum — a habit & focus tracker. Screens: (1) onboarding welcome with a short value prop and a "Get started" action, (2) Home dashboard: today's habits with check-off, streak counts, and a weekly completion bar chart, (3) Habit library: list with add/edit/delete through a bottom-sheet modal (name, emoji, target days per week), (4) Focus timer: pomodoro-style timer screen with a session log, (5) Insights: monthly completion heatmap plus per-habit trend rows, (6) Settings: dark/light theme toggle, daily reminder time, export data and clear data. Persist everything locally with AsyncStorage so it works fully offline. Expo Router tabs, TypeScript, dark mode by default.
