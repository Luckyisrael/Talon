# Brag Plan: Talon

## What is this app?

Talon is a browser IDE with an AI build agent that takes a plain-English description of a Solana Mobile app, plans it, writes real React Native/Expo code, verifies it through a typecheck → runtime → vision → interaction ladder (repairing its own failures), and streams it onto a live Android device — no VS Code, no Android Studio, no local toolchain.

## The angle

A **coming-soon launch teaser** for a product that is quietly already usable. The video plays like a blockbuster trailer — one declarative line per beat, black void, acid-lime strikes — but the punchline is honest: the trailer says "COMING SOON" and the fine print says *the Free tier is open today*. The joke/claim comes from the project's own waitlist page ("The waitlist is for Pro. Building is open now."), not from trying to be funny. This could only be Talon's teaser: the verify ladder, the plan card, and the lime-on-void instrument aesthetic are the product's own material.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

Black void. A single acid-lime dot sits inside a hairline rounded square — the actual Talon logo mark — breathing once. Then the first line slams in at display scale:

**"ONE SENTENCE."**

and a beat later, beneath it:

**"A SHIPPED APP."**

The hook earns the next 20 seconds by making a grand claim in the product's own visual language (hairline geometry, lime on near-black, tight-tracked Inter) before showing anything.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The plan card ticking.** The agent's approval card in the stream — 4 checklist rows completing one by one (`Scaffold screens` → `Wire MWA wallet` → `Typecheck + lint` → `Boot device`), mono metadata, lime "now" marker moving down.
- **The verify ladder flipping.** Four mono chips — `STATIC` → `RUNTIME` → `VISION` → `INTERACT` — flip to PASS in sequence, ending in a row of green. This is the "It won't ship broken code" claim made visible.
- **The phone going live.** The dark phone preview lights up with the running app and a `LIVE` lamp pulsing green — code on the left, a real Android target on the right, inside one browser page.

## Outro / punchline

Cut to void. The Talon mark and name slam in full-screen. Badge: `EARLY ACCESS`. Final line in the project's own voice:

**"COMING SOON."** — hold — **"Building is open now."**

Music swells and stops on the mark. The tease is real (Pro is genuinely coming soon) and the honest twist lands without winking.

## User flow worth showing

1. **Entry:** a prompt is typed into the composer — "Build a tip jar app with Solana wallet sign-in" (fictional stand-in content).
2. **Key action:** the plan card appears and its checklist ticks through; tool-trace rows stream beneath it; the verify ladder chips flip PASS one by one.
3. **Result:** the phone preview on the right lights up — the app running on a live Android target, `LIVE` lamp pulsing.

Scenes 2–4 carry this flow as the centerpiece; the landing-page hero copy appears only as framing.

- Interpretation: trailer-scale pacing — one short declarative claim per scene, giant tight-tracked type on the void, dramatic scale-in reveals and slow crossfades, restraint everywhere except the lime accent and the two or three big audio hits.

## Audio direction

- Role: cinematic support — a low steady bed with a swell into the outro and restrained motion-matched accents.
- Music: bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (steady, clean — the `polished`/`cinematic` track), ~109.96 BPM, cue preset available.
- Music treatment: starts at 0 with a ~0.8s fade-in under the hook, volume bed 0.30–0.35, builds through the flow scenes, swells at the outro slam (~17.5s), fast fade-out over the final 1s.
- Music cue guidance: preset at `<skill-dir>/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Strong cues to target: **8.74s** (plan-card scene land), **13.11s** (verify-ladder payoff / phone goes live), **17.47s** (outro mark slam). Beat-grid windows for sequential reveals: plan checklist rows near 8.7 / 9.8 / 10.9 / 12.0; verify chips near 13.1 / 14.2 / 15.3 — snap accents to the grid, but hold each text item ≥ its readability floor (flagged below).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass may let the lime dot, hero glow, and final logo presence breathe. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse-to-moderate, cinematic (2–3 big hits plus light accents): one deep bell on the reveal, soft impacts on scene transitions, light drops on checklist/chip arrivals, one resonant bell on the outro slam.
- Audio-coupled moments: hook lines slamming in (impact), plan checklist ticking row by row (light drops), verify chips flipping PASS (crisp accents), phone lamp going LIVE (bell), logo slam (heavy bell + swell).
- Restraint rule: no sound on every animation; never more than one SFX fighting the music at a time; no UI chatter during holds.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook: One sentence — 4s
Void `#08090a`. The Talon mark (hairline square + lime dot) fades in centered-small and breathes once. Display type slams in, one line at a time: **"ONE SENTENCE."** then **"A SHIPPED APP."** (mixed case allowed per cinematic heavy-tracking treatment; lines are short enough to read at the floor).
Sequential/interaction: yes — mark, then line 1, then line 2, each a fast slam + hold (line 1 holds ≥1.2s, line 2 holds ≥1.2s into the transition).
Audio intent: anticipation — quiet bed starts, first big accent lands with line 1.
Audio-coupled idea: line 1 slam on the strong cue near 0.56–1.0s; line 2 a beat later.
Music: cinematic bed fades in.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 2 (scale-in crossfade, 0.95 → 1.0).

### Scene 2 — Reveal: The browser IDE — 4s
The IDE window materializes on the hero-floor gradient: left = agent stream with composer, right = dark phone preview, hairline panel edges, mono gauge labels (`AGENT` / `DEVICE`). Small pill: `Talon-native AI coding platform`. A prompt types into the composer: *"Build a tip jar app with Solana wallet sign-in"* (fictional stand-in). Headline claim above or after: **"Watch it get written."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — window scales in, then the prompt types character-by-character, then the send button pulses.
Audio intent: the reveal — one deep bell as the window lands; soft key ticks under the typing.
Audio-coupled idea: typed prompt with subtle randomized keypress ticks; send pulse on a nearby beat.
Music: bed continues, energy lifts slightly.
Transition mood: dramatic → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The plan executes — 4s
Close on the stream. The plan card lands (`PLAN · 4 STEPS`) and its checklist ticks one by one: `Scaffold screens` → `Wire MWA wallet` → `Typecheck + lint` → `Boot device`. Tool-trace rows stream beneath (`write_file App.tsx`, `shell · gradle assemble`). Lime "now" marker moves down the list.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 checklist rows complete in order, one at a time. Each is a short label: reveal quickly, but hold each ≥0.8s settled and keep the full set on screen ≥1.2s at the end — snap arrivals to every other beat (~1.1s apart), not every beat.
Audio intent: methodical momentum — light drop/soft tick per completed row, nothing louder than the bed.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card/checklist sequence; first and last rows accented, middle rows quieter.
Music: bed continues; strong cue ~8.74s marks the card landing.
Transition mood: hard/dramatic → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Verify, then live on device — 4s
The verify ladder: four mono chips flip in sequence — `STATIC` `RUNTIME` `VISION` `INTERACT` — each landing PASS (pulse-green). Headline: **"It won't ship broken code."** (real project copy). As the fourth chip passes, the phone preview lights: a simple app screen appears, `LIVE` lamp pulses green, mono label `seeker · metro relay`.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 chips flip one by one (short labels: reveal fast, hold the set ≥1.2s after the last flips), then the phone screen turns on as the payoff.
Audio intent: rising confirmation — crisp accents on chip flips, one satisfying bell when the phone goes LIVE.
Audio-coupled idea: chip-by-chip sequence on beats (~13.1s strong cue for the first flip/payoff), phone-live bell as the climax.
Music: build toward the swell.
Transition mood: dramatic cut to black → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro: Coming soon — 4s
Void. The Talon mark + wordmark **"Talon"** slam in full-screen (the deep bell). Badge `EARLY ACCESS` fades up. Then the punchline pair, each with a real hold (≥1.2s): **"COMING SOON."** — small mono under it — **"Building is open now."** Final micro line: `20 prompts · 60 minutes device time · free` (real waitlist copy).
Sequential/interaction: yes — mark slam, badge, punchline line 1, punchline line 2.
Audio intent: the swell and stop — music peaks on the mark slam, decays under the lines, one final resonant bell, then out.
Audio-coupled idea: logo slam on the strong cue ~17.47s; line arrivals quiet.
Music: swell peaks at slam, 1s fade to silence.
Transition mood: end.
